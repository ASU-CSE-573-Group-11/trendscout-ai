At Sourcegraph, our mission is to bring clarity and control to the world's most complex codebases. We help teams deeply understand and evolve their code in a world of constant change.

We value...

Being direct & transparent

Our communication is direct, proactive, and transparent. We respect each other, trust in our ability to handle candid feedback, and disagree-and-commit when needed.

Winning together

We believe our best work happens when we support one another. We collaborate openly, help teammates succeed, and recognize that lasting impact comes from shared effort and shared wins.

High-agency

We take ownership and act, doing what's needed to deliver for our customers and holding ourselves accountable for outcomes.

Customer love

We put customers at the center of our decisions. We work backward from their real problems, earn trust through quality, and measure success by the value we deliver to them.

Top-tier perks for a world-class team

We hire the most talented people in the world and give them the support they need to do their best work while thriving outside of it, too. From flexible time off and generous travel budgets to professional growth support and wellness stipends, we invest in every part of your life, not just your work.
