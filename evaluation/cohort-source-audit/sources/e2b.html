Values

How we
work

  • High agency

    You see what needs doing and do it. No waiting for permission, no waiting for a spec. Ownership is the default, not the exception.

  • Ambition to ship

    We measure progress in things that are live, not things that are planned. Bias toward shipping, then iterating in the open.

  • Humility & curiosity

    Strong opinions, loosely held. The best argument wins regardless of who makes it. We stay curious about what we do not yet understand.

  • Developer experience

    Every call we make starts from what builders actually need. It is why a core part of our infra is open source: shipping in the open turns real user feedback into a faster, sharper product.
