Meet Pinecone

We’re a group of veteran experts in databases, cloud infrastructure, search, information retrieval, and AI. We collectively spent decades researching and building distributed data systems at companies like AWS, Databricks, Google, and Microsoft.

Our Core Values

We are a dedicated, open-minded, and kind group of people who love working together on incredibly challenging yet rewarding problems. At Pinecone you will work with world-class scientists and engineers who have built large scale ML applications and platforms at leading companies and cloud providers.

Outcomes

We measure our success based on customer value delivered. Real progress is what customers can do that they couldn't before, and we celebrate when they are using what we built.

Velocity

We decide with the information in hand and learn fast. Perfect information rarely arrives in time to matter. When a call doesn't pay off, we look at what we learned.

Creativity

We believe the best ideas don't come from one room or one title. The non-obvious moves come from anywhere in the company, and are sharpened where teams cross paths.

Engineered for the People Building Pinecone

Pinecone offers benefits designed to help you and your family live well, plan ahead, and do your best work.

Health and Wellness

Medical, dental, and vision plans, paid parental leave, mental health resources, infertility coverage, and access to Kindbody, Wellhub and Talkspace. Full coverage for you and your family, because creativity depends on people who are well supported.

Compensation and Financial Benefits

Competitive base salary and meaningful equity, so you share in the outcomes we deliver together. Retirement resources to grow long-term savings and income protection benefits are available to protect your family against the unexpected.

Other Benefits

Pinecone is a hybrid in-office and remote work environment that offers Flexible PTO, and a WFH equipment stipend so you can move at full velocity from anywhere. An annual company onsite and team offsites keep the whole system in sync.
