How we work

We're building the platform every agent will depend on.

Browserbase is a small team with a high bar. Move fast, take ownership, and work on things that actually matter. What you build here will ship, scale, and have immediate impact.

Come join us, do the most meaningful work of your career, and learn more than you thought possible along the way.

Our values

At Browserbase, we make the web programmable for agents.

This lofty goal entails a team that moves fast, owns outcomes, and solves problems most teams avoid.

These principles guide how we build: what we prioritize, how we make decisions, and what we expect from each other.

Boldly dare to trust

We create a culture of openness where trust empowers us to collaborate, innovate, and take calculated risks together.

Assume best intent

We approach each interaction with the belief that our teammates have positive intentions, fostering respect and understanding.

Disagree and commit

We express our perspectives openly, and once a decision is made, we unite in action to support our team’s goals.

Embrace your mistakes

We see mistakes as opportunities to learn, owning them with humility and using them as stepping stones to improve.

Thrive through growth

We commit to a growth mindset, viewing challenges as chances to evolve and strengthen ourselves and our team.

Pressure is a privilege

We recognize that high expectations are a testament to our potential, and we rise to the occasion, confident in our resilience.

Obsess over customers

We begin and end every decision with our customers in mind, staying relentlessly curious about how they work so we can help them win.
